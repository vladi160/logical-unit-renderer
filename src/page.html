Logical Unit Renderer

Every screen position is a unit that holds one color and one vector. Above the units sit pure functions in levels. A change flows down the graph and only the units it reaches are recomputed. The engine has no 3D in it: no xyz, no camera, no matrices, no physics.

What to do, and what to watch

  • Drag the cube. Only the groups around the cube recompute. The Shadow node runs and stops early, because a turning cube casts the same shadow.
  • Press JUMP. Gravity is one rule writing one state. The shadow shrinks and softens by a rule. Nothing traces light.
  • Press BOOM. The button sits bottom-left, and its vector chain ends at the cube. The 48 pieces and their shadows are regions of their own. Units between the button and the cube are never touched.
  • Drag the light sideways, then up and down. Sideways, the Sky and Floor nodes run and stop early. Up and down changes their colors, so every unit recomputes and the incremental mode has nothing to skip.
  • Switch the view. Vectors colors each unit by the region its one vector points at. Recomputed shows which units the last half second touched, over the 16×16 groups.
  • Turn off edge blending. One vector per unit means one owner per unit. Without a connector that mixes two regions in one unit, edges turn jagged.

Incremental against full redraw, same engine

Full redraw recomputes every unit every frame, the way a classic renderer redraws each frame. Both modes produce identical images. The difference is only what gets skipped.

What it shows

  • The model runs end to end: units with one vector, pure function nodes, connectors, levels, input going up and changes coming down.
  • Work follows the size of the change. When nothing changes, nothing runs.
  • Every unit can say why it has its color. Hover the scene to read the chain.

What it does not show

  • That shape information can disappear. Cube shape is four corners on an ellipse, which is an orthographic projection written in 2D. It works because the cube turns around one axis under a fixed view. A model that turns freely, like the soldier, needs its shape stored, computed or learned somewhere.
  • A win for global changes. A light that recolors the sky reaches every unit, and incremental is then no faster than full redraw.
  • GPU speed. This is single-threaded JavaScript, so read the ratio between the modes, not the milliseconds.
Source: core.js holds the engine and knows nothing about cubes or light. scene.js builds the scene from regions, objects and rules. app.js runs this page.
